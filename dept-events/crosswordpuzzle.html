Crossword Puzzle

Details

A word-based competition organized by the CSE & Allied Departments. Participants solve a themed crossword puzzle under a time limit that focuses on CS concepts, programming terms, and general knowledge.

Instructions

  • Format: Individual participants.
  • Each participant receive the same crossword puzzle.
  • Duration : 2-3 PM.
  • No external help or internet usage allowed—answers must be completed unaided.
  • Write answers clearly on the provided answer sheet or submit via the specified submission method.
  • Partial answers may receive partial credit per judging criteria.

Prizes

  • First Prize : 2000
  • Second Prize : 1000
