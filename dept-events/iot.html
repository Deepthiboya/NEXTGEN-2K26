IoT Hackathon — Rules & Guidelines

1. Eligibility

  • The hackathon is open to undergraduate and postgraduate students from Engineering disciplines.
  • Participants must register in teams (maximum 3 members per team).
  • Each team must have a unique team name and a designated team leader.

2. IoT Hackathon Themes

Participants must design solutions under any of the following themes:

i. AI–IoT Smart Home Applications for Environment and Sustainability

  • Home automation using voice commands (Alexa, Google Assistant integration)
  • Water quality and usage monitoring systems
  • Intruder and fire detection using AI-enabled sensors
  • Predictive maintenance of home appliances

ii. IoT Applications for Smart Health

  • Wearable health monitoring (heart rate, SpO₂, temperature)
  • Remote patient monitoring and alert systems
  • Smart medicine dispenser with reminder notifications
  • AI-IoT-based health data analytics for early disease prediction
  • IoT-enabled emergency response systems

iii. IoT Applications for Smart Agriculture

  • Precision Farming & Automated irrigation based on soil moisture and weather data
  • Crop disease detection using AI and IoT camera sensors
  • IoT-based fertilizer and pesticide monitoring systems
  • Soil Health Monitoring, Agri-Robotics

3. Registration Process

  • Teams must register using the official registration portal.
  • A brief idea/abstract (200 words) describing the proposed solution must be submitted at registration.

4. Project Guidelines

  • The project must involve IoT-based innovation integrating hardware (sensors, actuators, microcontrollers) and software (cloud, analytics, mobile/web interface).
  • Teams must bring their own hardware kits (Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, sensors, etc.) unless otherwise provided.
  • Internet access will be provided, but teams are responsible for software installation, library updates and device configuration.
  • IoT Theme based projects development should be completed during 11:00 AM to 03:00 PM.

5. Hackathon Phases

  1. Idea Submission Round (11:00 AM – 12:00 PM): Evaluation of submitted abstracts.
  2. Prototype Development Round (12:00 PM – 2:00 PM): Shortlisted teams build and demonstrate working models.
  3. Final Presentation (2:00 PM – 3:00 PM): Judges visit each team for the final presentation.
  4. Teams must manage their own time effectively for development, testing, and presentation.
  5. Awards will be given to the top-performing teams. Judges' decisions are final.

6. Evaluation Criteria (50 Points)

  • Innovation and Creativity: Uniqueness of the idea — 10 Points
  • Technical Feasibility and Project Execution: Integration of hardware and software — 10 Points
  • Impact and Relevance: Real-world relevance and social usefulness — 10 Points
  • Presentation and Teamwork: Clarity, teamwork, and communication — 10 Points
  • User Experience and Future Scope: Potential for further development or commercialization — 10 Points

Prizes

  • First Prize : 3000
  • Second Prize : 2000
  • Third Prize : 1000
