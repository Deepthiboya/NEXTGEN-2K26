TinkerCAD Masters

Details

TinkerCAD Masters is a design and modeling competition where participants use TinkerCAD to create innovative 3D designs, circuits, or simulations. The event tests creativity, technical skills, and proficiency with the TinkerCAD platform.

Format & Duration

  • Format:Individual participants or teams of 3 members.
  • Time Duration: 2–5 PM for design and submission.
  • Rounds may include designing, modeling, and presenting the project to judges.

Instructions

  • Participants must register before the event.
  • All design work must be completed in TinkerCAD; no external tools are allowed.
  • Ensure that designs are original and created by the participants.
  • Submit the project before the deadline specified by organizers.
  • Judging will be based on creativity, complexity, functionality, and presentation.

Prizes

  • First Prize : 3000
  • Second Prize : 2000
  • Third Prize : 1000
