Bridge Blitz

Details

Bridge Blitz is a Civil Engineering design challenge where participants build model bridges using provided materials. The bridges will be tested for load capacity, stability, and efficiency. This event promotes creativity, structural analysis, and teamwork skills.

Format & Duration

  • Format: Teams of up to 3 members.
  • Time limit: 12-3 PM.
  • Testing session will be conducted immediately after construction.

Instructions

  • All building materials will be provided by the organizers.
  • Participants must use only the materials given (no external materials allowed).
  • Judging will be based on load-bearing capacity, efficiency ratio, and innovation in design.
  • Teams must complete the bridge within the given time frame.
  • Judges decision will be final.

Prizes

  • First Prize : 3000
  • Second Prize : 2000
  • Third Prize : 1000
