Technical Quiz

Details

The Technical Quiz is designed to test participants’ knowledge of engineering concepts, programming, electronics, and general technology trends. Participants will answer questions in multiple rounds, promoting analytical thinking and quick problem-solving skills.

Format & Duration

  • Format: Individual participants.
  • Time Duration: 2-4 PM, including multiple rounds like buzzer, rapid-fire, and written questions.
  • Rounds will test technical knowledge, logical reasoning, and practical problem-solving.

Instructions

  • Teams/participants must register before the event starts.
  • No mobile phones or reference materials are allowed during the quiz.
  • Follow the quiz master's instructions carefully.
  • Judging will be based on correctness and speed of answers; organizers decisions are final.

Prizes

  • First Prize : 2000
  • Second Prize : 1000
