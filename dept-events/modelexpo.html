Model Expo

Details

The Model Expo is an exhibition event where participants showcase innovative models and projects. It provides a platform for students to demonstrate creativity, engineering skills, and technical knowledge.

Format & Duration

  • Format: Teams of 2–3 Mmembers or individual participants.
  • Duration: 12-2 PM.
  • Rounds may include multiple-choice questions, buzzer rounds, and rapid-fire rounds.

Instructions

  • Participants must register before the event.
  • Models should be original and made by the participants themselves.
  • Organizers may provide limited materials; participants can bring additional components if allowed.
  • Judging will be based on innovation, design, functionality, and presentation.
  • Judges decisions are final.

Prizes

  • First Prize : 3000
  • Second Prize : 2000
  • Third Prize : 1000
