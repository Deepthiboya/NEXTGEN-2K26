MindSpark Quiz

Details

MindSpark Quiz is a fast-paced competition designed to test logical reasoning, analytical skills, and general knowledge. Participants will answer questions across multiple domains including science, technology, and current affairs.

Format & Duration

  • Format: Teams of max 3 members or Individual participants.
  • Duration: 12-3 PM.
  • Rounds may include multiple-choice questions, buzzer rounds, and rapid-fire rounds.

Instructions

  • Teams/participants must register before the event starts.
  • No mobile phones or external help allowed during the quiz.
  • Follow the quiz master’s instructions carefully.
  • Judging is final and based on correctness and speed of answers.

Prizes

  • First Prize : 2000
  • Second Prize : 1000
