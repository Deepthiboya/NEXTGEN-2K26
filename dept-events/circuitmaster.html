Circuit Master Quiz

Details

Circuit Master Quiz is an electronics-focused competition testing participants’ knowledge of circuits, electronics components, devices, and problem-solving skills. The quiz evaluates both theoretical understanding and practical applications.

General Rules

  • Team Composition: Two members per team.
  • Language: Questions will be in English.
  • No Electronic Devices: Mobile phones, calculators, or smart devices are not allowed.
  • Disqualification: Misconduct, discussion during the quiz, or use of unfair means will lead to disqualification.
  • Judge’s Decision: The decision of the judges is final and binding.

Round 1: Preliminary Round (Written Test)

  • 30 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
  • Duration: 30 minutes.
  • Mode: Online (Google Form).
  • Selection: Top 50% of teams qualify for the next round.

Round 2: Conceptual Round

  • Type: Oral Q&A.
  • Format: Each team gets direct questions.
  • Elimination: 50% of teams move forward to the final round.

Round 3: Application & Problem Solving

  • Type: Circuit-based / Numerical questions.
  • Mode: Verbal explanation.
  • Elimination: Top teams qualify as winners.

Prizes

  • First Prize : 2000
  • Second Prize : 1000
