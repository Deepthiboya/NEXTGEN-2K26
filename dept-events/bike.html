Slow Bike Riding

Details

Slow Bike Riding is a skill-based race where participants must ride their bikes at the slowest possible speed while keeping the bike continuously moving. The rider who takes the maximum time to reach the finish line without violating any rules will be declared the winner.

Format & Duration

  • Format: Individual participation.
  • Time: 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM.
  • The race will be conducted on a designated track.
  • Only first gear is allowed.

Game Rules

  1. The race will be conducted on a designated track.
  2. The rider must maintain the slowest possible speed while keeping the bike moving.
  3. Only first gear is allowed.
  4. The rider must not put their feet on the ground.
  5. The rider must stay within the marked track boundaries.
  6. Stopping the bike completely is not allowed.
  7. Reversing the bike is not allowed.
  8. Overtaking or obstructing another participant is prohibited.
  9. Touching another rider or bike is strictly prohibited.
  10. Any fall or loss of control will result in disqualification.
  11. The rider who takes the maximum time to reach the finish line without violating any rule will be declared the winner.
  12. In case of a tie, a re-run will be conducted.
  13. The decision of the judges will be final and binding.

Registration Guidelines

  1. Registration is compulsory for all participants.
  2. Participants must wear helmets.
  3. Participants must have a valid driving licence.
  4. Only registered participants will be permitted to participate.
  5. Each participant will be allotted a registration/bib number.
  6. Participants must wear the allotted number visibly during the race.
  7. Registration is non-transferable; one participant cannot compete using another participant's registration.
  8. Participants must follow all event rules and safety instructions issued by the organizers.
  9. The organizers reserve the right to reject or cancel registration in case of incomplete or incorrect information.
  10. No on-the-spot registration will be permitted unless announced by the organizers.
